Good to know

Your first visit

Book or walk in

Online booking is open 24/7 and goes straight into the diary. Walk in if you'd rather — we'll fit you in when we can.

Patch test first

Any colour, highlights, keratin or Brazilian blowdry needs a patch test at least 48 hours before. Five minutes, £1.

Not sure what to book?

Book the £1 consultation. We'll talk through colour, length and what actually suits you before committing to anything.

Paying

Cash, credit card and debit card all fine in the salon. Online bookings are confirmed by email.
